# Community Guidelines for Conduct

These guidelines provide a safe, respectful, productive, and collaborative place for anyone contributing to Shakedown, where diversity of ideas is considered good. They apply to all collaborative spaces—community channels such as mailing lists, submitted patches, commit comments, issues, and pull requests.

* Participants are expected to be tolerant of opposing views.
* Participants must ensure that their language and actions are free from personal attacks and disparaging remarks.
* When interpreting the words and actions of others, participants should always assume good intentions.
* Behavior that could reasonably be considered harassment will not be tolerated.

If you believe these guidelines have been violated, you can email sebastianconcept@gmail.com or contact a repository maintainer on GitHub.
